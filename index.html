01 — About

What I actually do

Short version for the 30-second scan, detail below for the deep read.

I'm a B.E. Artificial Intelligence & Data Science engineering graduate who spends most of my time on the applied end of LLMs, the part where a model has to survive contact with a real database, a real support team and a real deployment environment.

At Vizpay Business Solutions I owned an internal AI support assistant end to end: building the training set, fine-tuning Qwen2.5-Coder 14B with QLoRA/PEFT, designing a dual-adapter intent-classifier + NL2SQL pipeline, writing the test cases, and containerising the whole thing for on-premise deployment — plus the version logs and change records that made it maintainable after I left.

Alongside that I build agentic systems (LangChain / LangGraph, async orchestration) and RAG pipelines over vector stores like Pinecone. I care a lot about evaluation: I'd rather report a measured 94% on a held-out set than a demo that only works on the happy path.

What I'm looking for: an AI/ML/Data Engineer role where I can own model pipelines end to end fine-tuning, retrieval, evaluation and deployment on a team that ships to users.
